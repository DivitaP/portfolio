About Me

Who am I?

I’m a Master’s student in Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder with two years of professional software development experience at TIAA. I helped to reduce client onboarding time by over 50% and optimized API performance using techniques such as parallelism, paging, and caching. My technical toolkit includes Java, Spring Boot, Python, AWS, Apache Kafka, and MongoDB, and I have a strong foundation in building scalable, asynchronous microservices. I’ve consistently been recognized for innovation and performance, receiving awards like the Titan Award and contributing to a CIO 100-winning project.

Beyond the workplace, I actively explore emerging technologies through hands-on projects in blockchain, natural language processing, and cybersecurity along with support of my full stack technical expertise. From developing a decentralized music platform on Ethereum, developing a proxy cache server and distributed file system from scratch to simulating cyber-attack defense mechanisms using reinforcement learning and doing in depth malware analysis, I enjoy translating complex concepts into impactful solutions. I bring leadership experience from internships and hackathons, a strong academic record, and a commitment to learning and growth—making me well-prepared to contribute meaningfully in a dynamic, tech-driven environment.
